# Cape Town Itinerary — 26–28 January 2027

## Purpose

Add this tentative Cape Town itinerary to the Southern Africa 2027 trip website.

The itinerary should feel **flexible rather than over-scheduled**. Activities can move slightly depending on weather, traffic, energy levels, and opening times.

### Important constraints

- Accommodation: **Mountain Marina Luxury Apartments, V&A Waterfront**
- Group will have its **own transport during the day**.
- Use **Uber / rideshare for nights out** rather than driving.
- **Do not add Cape Point.**
- **Do not move the final afternoon at the V&A Waterfront on 28 January.**
- **Do not move the final night out on Bree Street on 28 January.**
- Table Mountain should be treated as **weather dependent** and moved to the first suitable clear-weather opportunity if required.

---

# Tuesday, 26 January — Franschhoek → False Bay → Table Mountain → Waterfront

## Morning

### Leave Franschhoek

Check out and drive toward Cape Town via the False Bay side.

### Muizenberg + St James

Suggested activities:

- Walk along Muizenberg Beach.
- See the colourful beach huts.
- Continue through / stop briefly at St James.
- Keep this as a relaxed coastal stop rather than a long beach day.

### Kalk Bay — explore + lunch

Kalk Bay replaces Gordon's Bay in the original plan.

Suggested activities:

- Walk around the harbour and village.
- Browse local shops and galleries.
- Have lunch in Kalk Bay.
- Prioritise a relaxed seaside atmosphere and local character over a formal meal.

## Afternoon

### Table Mountain — weather permitting

If the mountain is clear and the cableway is operating:

- Drive to Table Mountain.
- Take the cableway up rather than making this a major hike day.
- Spend time enjoying the summit viewpoints.

**Weather rule:** Table Mountain should be done at the **first good clear-weather opportunity** during the Cape Town stay. If weather is poor on 26 January, skip it and move it to another suitable window.

If Table Mountain is not possible:

- Continue to Mountain Marina.
- Check in.
- Spend additional time exploring the V&A Waterfront.

## Evening

### Check in — Mountain Marina Luxury Apartments

Allow time to unpack and reset after the drive.

### V&A Waterfront

- Easy first-evening walk around the Waterfront.
- Keep the evening relaxed after the transfer from Franschhoek.

### Dinner — Time Out Market Cape Town

Use Time Out Market as an easy group dinner with a variety of food options.

---

# Wednesday, 27 January — Penguins + Coastal Road Trip + Sunset Cruise

This is the main scenic coastal driving day.

## Morning

### Leave V&A Waterfront

Suggested departure: **around 07:30**.

### Boulders Beach Penguin Colony

Suggested time: **08:30–09:45**.

- See the African penguins.
- Walk the boardwalks.
- Allow enough time to enjoy the area rather than treating it as a quick photo stop.

### Simon's Town

Suggested time: **09:50–10:45**.

- Short harbour / town walk.
- Coffee or snack.
- Browse the main area.

Do not turn this into another long stop because the coastal drive is the main experience of the day.

## Late morning / early afternoon

### Scenic coastal drive

Drive from Simon's Town through:

1. Red Hill
2. Scarborough
3. Misty Cliffs
4. Kommetjie
5. Noordhoek

This route is intended to provide a scenic Cape Peninsula experience **without visiting Cape Point**.

### Optional Noordhoek / Long Beach stop

Take a short beach or viewpoint stop if the group feels like it and timing is comfortable.

### Chapman's Peak Drive

Take time to enjoy the road rather than simply driving through it.

Suggested activities:

- Stop at viewpoints where safe and permitted.
- Take photos.
- Enjoy the mountain / ocean scenery.

## Lunch / early afternoon

### Hout Bay

Suggested time: **around 13:30–15:00**.

Activities:

- Explore the harbour.
- Have fish and chips for lunch.
- Look for seals around the harbour area.

A dedicated seal-island boat trip is **not required** unless the group specifically decides this is a priority.

## Afternoon

### Camps Bay

Suggested time: **around 15:45–17:00**.

Activities:

- Walk along Camps Bay Beach / promenade.
- Have an afternoon cocktail or drink overlooking the beach.
- Relax after the driving portion of the day.

### Return to Mountain Marina

Return to the accommodation for a quick freshen-up before the evening activities.

## Evening

### Oranjezicht City Farm Market

Suggested time: **around 17:45–18:45**.

Use the market as an early dinner / grazing stop.

### Sunset cruise — V&A Waterfront

Suggested check-in: **around 19:00**, subject to the selected cruise operator and final sunset schedule.

- Sunset cruise from the V&A Waterfront.
- Treat this as the main evening experience.

### Optional Waterfront drink afterward

If the group still has energy, have a casual drink around the Waterfront after the cruise.

Do not add another major activity to this night.

# Thursday, 28 January — Choice Morning + Bo-Kaap + Final Waterfront + Bree Street

The morning should be presented as a **choice between three different experiences**, depending on group interest, weather and energy.

## Morning — choose ONE

### Option A — Lion's Head

**Best for:** active travellers, views, outdoors.

- Start early.
- Hike in daylight.
- Allow roughly 3 hours including travel and the hike.
- Follow with breakfast / brunch.

Use a guide if the group prefers additional local knowledge or support.

### Option B — Kirstenbosch National Botanical Garden

**Best for:** scenery, gardens, easy walking, relaxed pace.

Suggested activities:

- Explore the main gardens.
- Walk the canopy walkway.
- Enjoy the mountain backdrop.

This is the least strenuous of the three morning options.

### Option C — Robben Island

**Best for:** South African history, Nelson Mandela and cultural context.

- Use an early ferry from the V&A Waterfront.
- Allow approximately 4 hours for the complete experience.
- Exact January 2027 ferry departure times must be confirmed closer to the trip.

Robben Island is more timetable dependent than the other options. If selected, keep lunch timing flexible rather than rushing the experience.

---

## Lunch / early afternoon — Bo-Kaap

### Explore Bo-Kaap

Suggested activities:

- Walk through the neighbourhood.
- Take in the architecture and history.
- Keep the visit focused on culture and food rather than only photo stops.

### Authentic Cape Malay lunch

Preferred options:

#### Option 1 — Bo-Kaap Kombuis

Prioritise dishes such as:

- Bobotie
- Denningvleis
- Bredie
- Cape Malay tasting-style dishes
- Malva pudding

#### Option 2 — Biesmiellah

Good alternative for classic Cape Malay dishes such as:

- Tomato bredie
- Bobotie
- Breyani
- Denningvleis

Allow approximately **12:30–14:30**, but this can shift later if Robben Island is selected.

# Weather / Flexibility Rules

## Table Mountain

Treat Table Mountain as the main weather-dependent activity.

Priority:

1. Do it on **26 January** if the mountain is clear and conditions are good.
2. If conditions are poor, move it to the next suitable clear-weather window.
3. If necessary, Table Mountain can replace the chosen morning activity on **28 January**.
4. For a first-time visitor, prioritise Table Mountain over Lion's Head if only one can be done.

## Lion's Head

- Only select this option in suitable weather.
- Hike in daylight.
- Do not schedule a sunset descent.

## Sunset Cruise

- Confirm the exact January departure time with the selected operator.
- Keep the Oranjezicht market visit before the cruise so the evening does not become rushed.

# Overall Experience Goal

The itinerary is designed to give a first-time Cape Town visitor a balanced experience of:

- iconic mountain scenery
- beaches and the Atlantic / False Bay coastline
- African penguins
- scenic road-trip driving
- Cape food and culture
- Cape Malay heritage
- the V&A Waterfront
- an optional history experience at Robben Island
- a relaxed sunset cruise
- Cape Town nightlife

Target experience quality: **approximately 4.8 / 5 for a first-time visitor**, while avoiding an overly rushed schedule.
